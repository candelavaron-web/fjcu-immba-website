  • Semesters 3–4第三、四學期

    Write, defend, graduate撰寫、口試、畢業

    Dual-degree students must finish the thesis before going abroad.計畫出國攻讀雙碩士的同學，必須於出國前完成論文。

Do I need to find a thesis advisor right away?考上之後需要馬上找論文指導老師嗎？

No. After the add/drop period of each semester the office announces advisor information, and you choose when to start. Students usually spend one semester shaping the topic and two more writing it with their advisor.不用。所辦於每學期加退選結束後公告論文指導老師相關訊息，同學可依自身規劃選擇跟上的時間點：通常一學期構思論文主題架構，接下來兩學期與指導老師定期討論並完成撰寫。

I did not study business. What do I have to do?大學非商管主修，需要補修什麼？

Economics, Business Management, Statistics and Accounting are prerequisites. Submit proof that you passed one of them before your first semester, or take one undergraduate course at FJCU during that semester. Confirm the course with the office first — prerequisite credits do not count towards graduation.經濟學、管理學、統計學、會計學為基礎課程。請於第一學期開學前提交曾修讀且及格之證明，否則須於第一學期至大學部補修一科。擬補修的課程須事先與所辦確認；補修通過之基礎課程不列入畢業學分。

Can I transfer credits from previous graduate studies?之前修過的碩士課程可以抵免嗎？

Yes, once. Apply to the registrar in your first semester, within one week of classes starting. Approval is subject to the imMBA chair.可以，並以一次為限。請於入學當年度當學期向註冊組提出申請，時間至開學後一週截止，並經學程主任審核。

Dual Master's Degree跨國雙碩士

Two ways to earn your MBA兩種取得 MBA 的方式

Every imMBA student chooses between a two-year degree at Fu Jen or a 1+1 dual master's degree: the first year in Taipei, the second at a partner school abroad.每位同學都可以選擇在輔大完成兩年碩士，或參加 1+1 跨國雙碩士：碩一在台北，碩二前往海外合作學校。

Two-year imMBA at Fu Jen輔大兩年碩士

42 credits including a 6-credit thesis, normally completed in three to four semesters — with the option of a tuition-free exchange semester abroad.最低畢業學分 42 學分（含論文 6 學分），一般 3–4 個學期完成，並可申請姊妹校免學費交換。
