Courses & Requirements課程與修業規則

Management fundamentals, plus the skills AI is making essential管理基礎，加上 AI 時代的必備能力

The curriculum covers international business management, marketing, human resources, global supply chain, finance and service design, and adds the digital and data skills that cross-border companies now expect.課程涵蓋國際企業管理、行銷、人力資源、全球供應鏈管理、財務管理與服務設計，並融入智慧營運與數位創新等產業趨勢。

42 credits42 學分

Minimum for graduation, including a 6-credit thesis.最低畢業學分，含論文 6 學分。

3–4 semesters3–4 個學期

Typical time to complete the degree.一般完成學業所需時間。

New this year本學年新課

AI-Assisted Digital Product Development

Turn a business idea into a working digital product with AI: product design, web development and data management — no coding background required.運用 AI 將商業點子化為可操作的數位產品：產品設計、網頁建置與資料管理，無需程式基礎。

New this year本學年新課

Data Analytics for International Business

From data collection and visualization to statistics and machine learning, applied to markets, industries and cross-border operations.從資料蒐集、視覺化到統計分析與機器學習，應用於市場、產業與跨國營運情境。

Prerequisites基礎課程

Economics, Business Management, Statistics and Accounting. Non-business graduates prove or complete one before the second semester.經濟學、管理學、統計學、會計學。非商管背景新生須於第一學期提出證明或補修一科。

Thesis論文

Advisor lists are released in late March and early October. Enrol in “Thesis” before the add/drop deadline of the semester you plan to defend.每學期 3 月底及 10 月初公告教師群名單；擬口試當學期須先加選「論文」。

Course waivers學分抵免

One application only, due within the first week of classes and subject to the imMBA chair's approval.以一次為限，申請至開學後一週截止，並經學程主任審核。

Graduation畢業與離校

Thesis defense and leaving procedures論文口試與離校流程

You may apply for the thesis defense in any semester, once you meet the study requirements. Plan ahead: there are mandatory steps before and after the defense.符合修業規則即可於任一學期申請論文口試。口試前後皆有必要程序，請提前規劃辦理。

42 credits42 學分

Minimum for graduation, including a 6-credit thesis, prerequisites, required and elective courses and the English threshold.最低畢業學分（含論文 6 學分），另有基礎課程、專業必修選修與英文畢業門檻之規定。

Defense deadlines口試截止日

Fall semester: January 31. Spring semester: July 31. Exact dates follow the FJCU academic calendar.Fall 為每年 1 月 31 日、Spring 為每年 7 月 31 日，實際日期依當年度輔大行事曆。

Enrol in “Thesis”先加選「論文」

To keep the right to defend and graduate, enrol in “Thesis” before the add/drop deadline of that same semester.擬申請口試及畢業的當學期，必須於選課期間先加選「論文」乙科。
