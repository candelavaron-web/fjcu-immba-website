Role → permission → what you can do角色 → 權限 → 可執行的操作

The same person can be all three at once: you may sell your old textbook and buy a bicycle on the same afternoon. What changes is not the person, it is the permission — and it is enforced by the database, not by hiding buttons. 同一個人可以同時具備三種身分：可以賣掉舊教科書，也可以買一台自行車。改變的不是人，而是權限──而權限由資料庫把關，不只是把按鈕藏起來。

The life of one order一筆訂單的生命週期

  1. Waiting for payment待付款 The buyer placed the order and the stock is already reserved. Either side can still cancel. 買家已下單，庫存同時保留。此階段雙方皆可取消。
  2. Ready to ship待出貨 The seller confirmed the money arrived. From here on the record is kept, never deleted. 賣家確認已收到款項。此後紀錄只保存，不刪除。
  3. Shipped已出貨 The seller handed the item over or sent it. 賣家已交付或寄出商品。
  4. Completed已完成 The buyer confirms it arrived. The transaction becomes part of the sales history. 買家確認收到，交易成為銷售紀錄的一部分。
